Dispatch is a data-driven logistics optimization framework designed to model and improve the end-to-end decision-making process behind Indian e-commerce delivery networks. Rather than treating logistics as a single prediction problem, the system combines machine learning, graph algorithms, evolutionary optimization, clustering, forecasting, and risk classification across five interconnected decisions: predicting delivery times, optimizing multi-stop routes, locating regional warehouse hubs, forecasting shipment demand, and identifying high-risk dispatches. Its central research finding is that route sequencing, rather than shortest-path calculation itself, represents a major source of untapped delivery efficiency, with a custom Genetic Algorithm reducing multi-stop route distance by 19.8–33.7% against the fixed-order baseline used in the study. The framework is grounded in three heterogeneous real-world logistics datasets and is designed so that production shipment data can replace the research training data without restructuring the pipeline.
